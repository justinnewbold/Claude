# 🏛️ VAULT 13 - SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

**The Main Game: A Post-Apocalyptic Vault Management Simulation**

---

## Overview

Welcome to **Vault 13**, the flagship survival management game inspired by Fallout Shelter! Unlike the philosophical mini-games in this collection, Vault 13 is a full-featured management simulation where you take on the role of an Overseer, responsible for managing an underground vault and ensuring the survival of its dwellers in a post-nuclear wasteland.

## Game Concept

After the Great War devastated the surface world, humanity's last hope lies in underground vaults. As the Overseer of Vault 13, you must:

- **Manage Resources**: Keep power, water, and food flowing
- **Build & Expand**: Construct new rooms and facilities
- **Assign Workers**: Match dwellers to jobs based on their SPECIAL stats
- **Survive Events**: Respond to raiders, fires, infestations, and more
- **Keep Dwellers Happy**: Maintain morale and health
- **Grow Your Population**: Welcome new arrivals and expand

## Core Mechanics

### 📊 Resources

Your vault runs on three critical resources plus currency:

1. **⚡ Power** (Yellow)
   - Powers all vault systems
   - Generated by Power Generator rooms
   - Consumed by dwellers and facilities

2. **💧 Water** (Cyan)
   - Essential for dweller survival
   - Produced by Water Treatment rooms
   - Critical for health

3. **🍖 Food** (Orange)
   - Keeps dwellers fed and happy
   - Produced by Diner rooms
   - Affects dweller morale

4. **💰 Caps** (Currency)
   - Used to build new rooms
   - Used to heal injured dwellers
   - Found through random events

**Resource Storage**: Each resource has a maximum capacity that can be increased by building Storage Rooms.

**Critical Levels**: When resources drop below 20% capacity, dweller happiness and health suffer. If critical resources reach zero, game over!

### 👥 Dwellers

Each dweller has the classic **SPECIAL** stats system:

- **S**trength - Best for Power Generators
- **P**erception - Best for Water Treatment
- **E**ndurance - Affects health and stamina
- **C**harisma - Affects overall vault happiness
- **I**ntelligence - Best for Science Labs
- **A**gility - Best for Diners
- **L**uck - Affects random event outcomes

**Dweller Attributes**:
- **Health**: 0-100% (affected by events, resource shortages)
- **Happiness**: 0-100% (affected by job assignment, vault conditions)
- **Assignment**: Can be assigned to production rooms

### 🏗️ Room Types

**Production Rooms**:

1. **Power Generator** (⚡)
   - Cost: 150 caps
   - Produces: 5 power per turn (base)
   - Capacity: 2 workers
   - Best stat: Strength

2. **Water Treatment** (💧)
   - Cost: 120 caps
   - Produces: 5 water per turn (base)
   - Capacity: 2 workers
   - Best stat: Perception

3. **Diner** (🍖)
   - Cost: 100 caps
   - Produces: 5 food per turn (base)
   - Capacity: 2 workers
   - Best stat: Agility

**Utility Rooms**:

4. **Living Quarters** (🏠)
   - Cost: 100 caps
   - Increases population capacity
   - Capacity: 4 dwellers
   - Required for population growth

5. **Storage Room** (📦)
   - Cost: 80 caps
   - Increases max storage for all resources (+20 each)
   - Essential for late-game expansion

6. **Training Room** (💪)
   - Cost: 200 caps
   - Train dwellers to improve stats
   - Capacity: 2 trainees
   - Future feature

7. **Medbay** (⚕️)
   - Cost: 150 caps
   - Heal injured dwellers automatically
   - Capacity: 2 patients
   - Faster healing than manual

8. **Science Lab** (🔬)
   - Cost: 250 caps (most expensive)
   - Research new technologies
   - Capacity: 2 scientists
   - Best stat: Intelligence
   - Future feature

**Production Bonuses**:
- Each assigned worker increases production by 20%
- Room level (future feature) further multiplies output
- Matching dweller stats to room requirements increases efficiency

### 🎲 Random Events

Every turn has a 20% chance of triggering a random event:

**Positive Events**:
- **New Arrivals** 👤: New dwellers join your vault
- **Resource Discovery** ✨: Find extra power, water, food, and caps
- **Skill Improvement** 📈: Random dweller gains +1 to a SPECIAL stat

**Negative Events**:
- **Raider Attack** 💀: Dwellers lose health and happiness
- **Fire Outbreak** 🔥: Room catches fire, stops production
- **Rad Roach Infestation** 🪳: Room incident, stops production

**Managing Incidents**:
- Fires and infestations stop room production until resolved
- Currently auto-resolve after one turn
- Future: Manual intervention required

### ⏰ Turn System

Each turn represents **one day** in the vault:

**Turn Processing**:
1. **Production Phase**: All active rooms produce resources
2. **Consumption Phase**: Dwellers consume power (1), water (1), food (1) each
3. **Event Phase**: 20% chance of random event
4. **Happiness Update**: Dweller morale adjusts based on conditions
5. **Health Check**: Injuries and resource shortages apply damage

**Resource Balance**:
- Early game: 4 dwellers consume 4 power/water/food per turn
- Production must exceed consumption to stockpile reserves
- Build strategically to maintain positive resource flow

## 🎮 Controls & Commands

### Main Menu Actions

- **[B] Build Room**: Construct new facilities
- **[D] Manage Dwellers**: Heal, train, or unassign dwellers
- **[R] Assign Workers**: Assign idle dwellers to production rooms
- **[E] End Turn**: Advance to next day (processes all game systems)
- **[V] View Details**: See detailed stats for dwellers, rooms, events
- **[S] Save Game**: Save current progress to `vault_save.json`
- **[Q] Quit Game**: Exit (prompts to confirm)

### View Details Submenu

- **[1] View All Dwellers**: See complete dweller roster with SPECIAL stats
- **[2] View All Rooms**: See all vault facilities and their production
- **[3] View Event History**: Review last 20 events

### Manage Dwellers Submenu

- **[1] Unassign Dweller**: Remove dweller from their current room
- **[2] Heal Dweller**: Pay 50 caps to restore 50 health
- **[3] Train Dweller**: (Future feature) Improve specific stats

## 🎯 Strategy Guide

### Starting Strategy

Your vault begins with:
- **4 dwellers** with random SPECIAL stats
- **500 caps** starting currency
- **3 floors unlocked** with some pre-built rooms:
  - 1x Power Generator (Floor 1)
  - 1x Water Treatment (Floor 1)
  - 1x Living Quarters (Floor 2)
  - Several empty slots

**First Steps**:
1. Assign your 2 idle dwellers to work (match their best stats to rooms)
2. Build a Diner on Floor 1 (food is critical!)
3. Assign a dweller with high Agility to the Diner
4. End a few turns to generate resources and caps
5. Build a second Power Generator or Water Treatment based on resource consumption

### Mid-Game Strategy

**Population Growth**:
- New dwellers arrive via random events
- Build Living Quarters to increase population cap
- More dwellers = more consumption AND more production

**Resource Balance**:
- Monitor your resource bars each turn
- Keep resources above 50% capacity
- Build Storage Rooms when resources frequently max out

**Room Optimization**:
- Fully staff high-priority rooms first (Power, Water, Food)
- Assign dwellers to rooms matching their highest SPECIAL stat
- Unassign and reassign as better-suited dwellers arrive

### Late-Game Strategy

**Expansion**:
- You can build up to 10 floors
- Each floor can have 3 rooms
- Maximum vault size: 30 rooms

**Advanced Rooms**:
- Build Medbays to auto-heal injured dwellers
- Science Labs for future research features
- Training Rooms to improve dweller stats over time

**Event Management**:
- Keep caps reserve for healing after raider attacks
- Maintain high happiness to improve productivity
- Fires and infestations resolve themselves but cost 1 turn of production

## 💾 Save/Load System

**Auto-Save**: The game does NOT auto-save. You must manually save!

**Saving**:
- Press [S] at any time during the main menu
- Game saves to `vault_save.json` in the game directory
- Stores: day count, resources, dwellers, vault layout, recent events

**Loading**:
- On game start, checks for `vault_save.json`
- If found, prompts: "Saved game found! Load it? (y/n)"
- Press 'y' to continue your vault, 'n' to start fresh

**Save File Location**: `vault_save.json` (same directory as game)

## 🏆 Win/Loss Conditions

### Game Over Conditions

You lose if:
1. **All dwellers die** (health reaches 0 from starvation, radiation, attacks)
2. **Critical system failure** (both Power AND Water reach 0 simultaneously)

### Victory Conditions

Currently, the game is **endless survival**. Your goal is to:
- Survive as many days as possible
- Grow your population
- Maximize vault happiness
- Build a thriving underground civilization

**High Score**: Days survived + final population + caps remaining

## 📝 Technical Details

**File**: `vault_shelter.py`
**Lines of Code**: ~1,200
**Dependencies**: Python 3.7+ (standard library only)
**Save Format**: JSON
**Platform**: Cross-platform (Linux, macOS, Windows)

**Classes**:
- `VaultGame`: Main game controller
- `Dweller`: Individual vault resident
- `Room`: Vault facility
- `Resources`: Resource management
- `RoomType`: Enum for room types
- `RoomStats`: Configuration dataclass

**Design Pattern**: MVC-inspired architecture
- Model: Dweller, Room, Resources dataclasses
- Controller: VaultGame class with game loop
- View: Terminal-based UI with ANSI colors

## 🎭 Philosophical Connection

While this is the "main game" compared to the philosophical mini-games, Vault 13 still explores thought-provoking themes:

- **Utilitarianism**: Do you sacrifice individual dweller happiness for vault survival?
- **Resource Ethics**: Who gets resources when supplies run low?
- **Leadership**: What kind of Overseer will you be - benevolent or pragmatic?
- **Survival vs. Thriving**: Is mere survival enough, or do you owe dwellers happiness?

The mini-games explore abstract philosophical puzzles; Vault 13 lets you live those ethical dilemmas in a simulated society.

## Tips & Tricks

1. **Match Stats to Rooms**: Strength dwellers in Power, Perception in Water, Agility in Food
2. **Build Storage Early**: Don't waste generated resources by hitting the cap
3. **Save Before Major Decisions**: Experiment with building layouts
4. **Event Preparation**: Keep dwellers healthy for raider attacks
5. **Balance Population**: More dwellers = more production AND consumption
6. **Don't Rush**: Take time to understand resource flows before expanding
7. **Happiness Matters**: Happy dwellers work better and stay healthier
8. **Reserve Caps**: Always keep 100+ caps for healing emergencies
