# ECHO CHAMBERS

## A Quantum Narrative Game Unlike Anything You've Played

### What Makes This Game Unique?

**Echo Chambers** is a terminal-based narrative experience where you play as a consciousness that exists across multiple parallel timelines simultaneously. This isn't just branching narrative - it's something entirely new:

#### Revolutionary Mechanics

1. **Simultaneous Timeline Evolution**
   - All parallel realities continue to evolve even when you're not experiencing them
   - Timelines decay over time if neglected, becoming corrupted
   - Each timeline has dynamic properties that drift based on your absence

2. **Quantum Echo System**
   - Leave "echoes" (actions/influences) in one timeline that probabilistically propagate to others
   - Echoes have different propagation chances and can create cascading effects
   - Build networks of influence across realities

3. **Timeline Entanglement**
   - Create quantum connections between separate timelines
   - Entangled timelines affect each other in unpredictable ways
   - Risk and reward of linking fates across realities

4. **Convergence Events**
   - Random moments where timelines collide and one must be sacrificed
   - Make impossible choices about which realities deserve to exist
   - No two playthroughs will have the same convergences

5. **Memory Fragment Collection**
   - Piece together the truth about who you are across timelines
   - Each fragment reveals more about the nature of your fractured existence
   - Multiple endings based on whether you collect all memories

6. **Meaningful Consequences**
   - Every choice affects timeline properties (technology, society, environment, consciousness)
   - Properties influence what events can occur
   - Create feedback loops across your decisions

### The Story

You are a consciousness that exists across parallel timelines. You don't know how this happened or why, but you sense that something is wrong. Your memories are fragmented. The timelines are decaying. And a mysterious "Convergence" is approaching.

As you navigate between realities, leaving echoes and collecting memories, you'll uncover the truth about your existence - and face an impossible choice about your future.

### How to Play

```bash
python3 echo_chambers.py
```

The game is entirely text-based with rich terminal colors and effects. Navigate through choices, switch between timelines, manage decay, and watch as your decisions ripple across probability space.

### Features

- **Dynamic narrative generation** based on timeline state
- **Beautiful terminal UI** with color-coded timelines
- **Emergent gameplay** from interacting systems
- **Multiple endings** based on your choices
- **Replayability** - every playthrough is different due to procedural events and random convergences
- **Philosophical themes** about identity, consciousness, and choice

### Why It's Unique

Most games with "branching paths" just save your choices and show you different content. **Echo Chambers** actually simulates parallel realities that continue existing and evolving independently. You're not just choosing a path - you're managing an entire multiverse.

The combination of:
- Real-time timeline evolution
- Probabilistic echo propagation
- Random convergence events
- Dynamic property systems
- Memory fragment collection

...creates a gameplay experience that has never been done before.

### Requirements

- Python 3.6+
- Terminal with ANSI color support
- A mind ready to be fragmented across infinite possibilities

### The Philosophy

This game asks: If you could exist in multiple realities simultaneously, would you? And if those realities started to collapse, which versions of yourself would you save?

It's a game about:
- Identity across possibility space
- The cost of infinite choice
- Consciousness as a wave function
- The inevitable entropy of existence

### Multiple Endings

- **The Convergence** - Collapse all timelines into one, become whole
- **The Fracture** - Remain split across infinite realities forever
- **Dissolution** - Lose all timelines and fade into quantum uncertainty
