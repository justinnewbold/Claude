## 🆕 The 9 New Systems

### OPTION A - FULL RPG EXPERIENCE

1. **💕 Relationships & Breeding System**
   - Dwellers form partnerships and have children
   - Children inherit parent stats (averaged with variation)
   - Children grow up in 30 days
   - Family trees and genealogy tracking
   - 10-day pregnancy system

2. **🔬 Research & Tech Tree**
   - 20+ technologies to unlock
   - Science Labs and Archives generate research points
   - Tech bonuses: +20% production, automation, combat boosts
   - Prerequisites and unlockable room types
   - Progressive tech tiers (Basic → Advanced → Elite)

3. **🏛️ Vault Policies & Government**
   - 3 government types: Democracy, Autocracy, Technocracy
   - Government-specific policies
   - Influence resource for enacting policies
   - Policy effects: happiness, production, efficiency
   - Radio Rooms generate influence

### OPTION B - DYNAMIC WORLD

4. **💰 Trading & Merchant System**
   - Merchants visit every 15-20 days
   - Buy and sell equipment
   - Dynamic pricing
   - Merchant stays for 3 days
   - Special rare items only from traders

5. **🏴 Faction System**
   - 5 wasteland factions with unique personalities
   - Reputation system (-100 to +100)
   - Faction events affect relationships
   - Allied/Neutral/Hostile status
   - Faction-specific quests (planned)

6. **💀 Major Disasters & Crises**
   - Rare catastrophic events (1% chance/turn)
   - Types: Plague, Flood, Meltdown, Invasion, Famine
   - Multi-day duration with ongoing effects
   - Resolution mechanics (skills-based)
   - Disaster survival tracking

### OPTION C - MASSIVE CONTENT

7. **🏗️ 6 New Room Types**
   - 📻 Radio Room - Attracts dwellers, generates influence
   - 🔧 Workshop - Crafts equipment, gathers materials
   - ⚔️ Armory - Stores weapons, trains combat
   - 🌱 Garden - Alternative food (no power needed!)
   - 🏋️ Gym - Trains stats 50% faster
   - 📚 Archives - Generates research points

8. **🔧 Crafting System**
   - Craft advanced equipment from materials
   - 3 material types: Metal, Electronics, Cloth
   - Workshops generate materials
   - Craftable weapons and armor
   - Recipe requirements and tech prerequisites

9. **🌍 Seasonal Calendar**
   - 4 seasons: Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter
   - 25 days per season
   - Season-specific effects on production
   - Winter: -20% food, +10% power consumption
   - Summer: +20% food production

## 🔬 How Systems Interact

The 9 v5.0 systems create **emergent gameplay** through interactions:

**Example Gameplay Loop:**
1. **Breeding** → New dwellers born with inherited stats
2. **Research** → Unlock Workshop tech
3. **Workshop** → Gather materials
4. **Crafting** → Create advanced weapons
5. **Expeditions** → Armed dwellers find more loot
6. **Trading** → Sell excess for caps
7. **Policies** → Enact production bonuses
8. **Seasons** → Adapt to Winter food shortage
9. **Factions** → Build relationships for future benefits

All 25+ features work together in a **unified simulation**!
